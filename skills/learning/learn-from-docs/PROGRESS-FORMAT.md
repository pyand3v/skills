# PROGRESS.md Format

`PROGRESS.md` is the concise current-state and next-steps record for one source study. It is not a history of every session; durable changes in understanding belong in learning records.

## Template

```md
# {Source title} Progress

## Prior knowledge
- {Relevant experience, related reading, current confidence, or known misconception.}

## Agreed scope
{A short restatement of the active scope.}

## Covered sections
- [x] {Section}: {one-line takeaway}
- [ ] {Next section}: {why it is next}

## Takeaways
- **Introduced** — {concept or claim}
- **Practiced** — {concept or claim and evidence}
- **Demonstrated** — {concept or claim and evidence}

## Next study step
{The smallest useful next section, recall prompt, or application task.}

## Open questions
- {A question that needs more study, clarification, or a supplemental source}
```

## Rules

- Keep only the current scope, completed sections, active takeaways, next step, and unresolved questions.
- Record only source-relevant prior knowledge that affects the study path; preserve durable prior-knowledge evidence in a learning record when it changes future sessions.
- Mark a takeaway demonstrated only after a correct explanation, successful application, or completed exercise.
- Prefer the next one or two bounded sections over a fixed long-term curriculum.
- Move superseded history to a learning record rather than accumulating it here.
