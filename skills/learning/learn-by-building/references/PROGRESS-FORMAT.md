# PROGRESS.md Format

`PROGRESS.md` is the concise current state of a build-learning project, not a session transcript.

## Template

```md
# {Project name} Progress

## Prior knowledge
- {Relevant experience, confidence, or known misconception.}

## Capabilities
- **Introduced** — {concept or technique}
- **Practiced** — {concept or technique and evidence}
- **Demonstrated** — {concept or technique and evidence}

## Completed increments
- [x] 0001 {increment}: {verified outcome}

## Next increments
- [ ] {smallest valuable next behavior and why}
- [ ] {possible follow-on, only if useful}

## Blockers and open questions
- {A real decision, risk, or missing knowledge.}
```

## Rules

- Keep at most the next two to four likely increments; revise rather than accumulating a fixed plan.
- Mark demonstrated only after a correct explanation, successful application, or verified behavior.
- Move durable evidence or superseded history to a learning record rather than turning this into a log.
