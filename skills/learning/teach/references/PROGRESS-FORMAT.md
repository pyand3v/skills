# PROGRESS.md Format

`PROGRESS.md` is the current, concise view of one topic's learning state. It helps choose the next lesson without reconstructing the whole history. It complements `learning-records/`: records preserve evidence and changes over time, while progress shows what matters now.

## Template

```md
# {Topic} Progress

## Baseline
- {Relevant experience, current confidence, and known gaps.}

## Capabilities
- **{Capability}** — introduced — {Brief evidence or context.}
- **{Capability}** — practiced — {Brief evidence or context.}
- **{Capability}** — demonstrated — {Brief evidence or context.}

## Next likely lessons
1. {The most relevant bounded lesson}
2. {Another likely lesson}

## Recommended next step
{The single most appropriate action for the next session.}

## Open questions
- {A question that materially affects what to teach next.}
```

## Rules

- Keep this a current snapshot, not a session log or a long curriculum.
- Record only the next two to four likely lessons, and name one recommended next step.
- Update capabilities only after evidence: a completed exercise, correct explanation, successful check, or explicit learner confirmation.
- Use `introduced` for a capability the learner can recognize or explain, `practiced` for one they have applied with feedback, and `demonstrated` for one they can use successfully in an appropriate task.
- Capture durable evidence, prior knowledge, misconceptions, and mission changes in `learning-records/` rather than duplicating them here.
- Remove stale next steps and resolved open questions as the learner progresses.
