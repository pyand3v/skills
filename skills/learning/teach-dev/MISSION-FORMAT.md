# MISSION.md Format

`MISSION.md` lives in a topic directory. It captures why the learner is pursuing that topic and grounds choices about lessons, exercises, and sources.

## Template

```md
# Mission: {Topic}

## Why
{One to three sentences describing the concrete outcome the learner wants in work or life.}

## Success looks like
- {A specific, observable capability}
- {Another specific, observable capability}

## Constraints
- {Time, prior commitments, project context, or learning preferences}

## Out of scope
- {An adjacent topic that should not distract from this mission}
```

## Rules

- Keep one focused mission per topic workspace.
- Prefer concrete outcomes over goals such as “understand X.”
- Clarify a vague mission before creating a learning path.
- Update the mission when the learner's goal changes, then write a learning record explaining the change.
- Keep it short enough to act as a compass rather than a curriculum.
