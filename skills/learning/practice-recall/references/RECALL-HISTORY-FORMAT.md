# RECALL.md Format

`RECALL.md` is a compact topic-level history of retrieval-practice sessions. It complements a workspace's progress and learning records: recall history captures what was tested and when to revisit it, while those files retain current capability state and durable evidence.

## Template

```md
# {Topic} Recall History

## 2026-09-02

### Concepts tested
- {Concept or capability}
- {Concept or capability}

### Outcome
- Strong: {what the learner retrieved or applied accurately}
- Partial: {what needed a correction}

### Suggested revisit
{Sooner | normal interval | later} — {the reason for this suggestion}
```

## Rules

- Append one brief dated entry per recall round; do not rewrite earlier outcomes.
- Record only concepts actually tested and feedback actually given.
- Use qualitative revisit guidance. Do not claim that a reminder was scheduled or that a fixed schedule is scientifically precise.
- Keep demonstrated-capability evidence in the workspace's progress or learning records instead of duplicating it here.
